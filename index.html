Methodology

How the Travel Ranking Was Calculated

The analysis covers regular-season travel for 138 Football Bowl Subdivision teams in 2025 and 2026.

Step 1

Build Each Team's Travel Schedule

Road and neutral-site games were taken from CollegeFootballData. Ordinary road teams travel from their home stadium to the game venue, while both teams travel to a neutral site.

Step 2

Match Home and Game Venues

Home stadiums, game venues, and geographic coordinates were matched with CollegeFootballData team and venue records.

Step 3

Calculate Round-Trip Distance

Straight-line distance between stadium coordinates was calculated with the Haversine formula, then doubled to represent the outbound and return journey.

Step 4

Measure Travel Conditions

A trip counts as long-haul when the destination is more than 1,000 miles from the team's home stadium. Road games no more than nine days apart count as back-to-back.

Step 5

Rank Teams by Total Miles

Teams are ranked from highest to lowest by their total round-trip travel miles across all 2026 road and neutral-site games.

Important Context

What the Mileage Does Not Show

The figures represent straight-line estimates. They do not reproduce exact flight paths, driving routes, connections, or a team's actual travel arrangements.

Source: Schedules, team records, venue records, and coordinates were taken from CollegeFootballData. Both seasons use the same source and calculation rules.
